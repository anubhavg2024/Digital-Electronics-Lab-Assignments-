The minutes counter advances when the seconds counter holds 59, and the hours
counter advances only when both seconds and minutes hold 59. The carry output of
each counter, high at its maximum value, provides this enable. Presetting the
counters near the boundary made the rollover visible without waiting 24 hours.
Counter values above the maximum (60 to 63, and 24 to 31) are unused.
